Ask Me Anything →

Do I need to know anything about tech?

+
Not at all. If you use email and Google Sheets, that's enough. I handle everything — the build, the connections, the testing. You just tell me what's wasting your time.

What if it breaks or stops working?

+
On the Monthly Support plan, I fix it at no extra cost — that's the whole point. On a one-off build, I'll still help. Most issues take minutes. I don't disappear after handover.

How long does setup actually take?

+
About a week from when we agree on what to build. I'll need access to your email and Google Sheets, and about 30 minutes of your time for the walkthrough. After that, it runs itself.

Will the AI replies sound weird or robotic?

+
No — try the live demo above and see for yourself. Replies are plain, professional English using your business name. You approve the tone before anything goes live.

What AI is used? Is it safe?

+
I use Anthropic's Claude AI — one of the leading AI models. Customer data is processed in real-time and sent to your own accounts. Nothing is stored on my servers. Fully compliant with the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

What tools does it connect to?

+
Most commonly Gmail, Google Sheets, Xero, and Facebook. I use Make (formerly Integromat) to connect everything. If you use something specific, ask me — it probably works.

Who can see my customer data?

+
Only you. Everything runs through your own accounts — your Gmail, Xero, Sheets. I build the connections but your data stays in your systems. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel and keep what was built?

+
Yes. You own everything. Cancel Monthly Support anytime and the automations keep running. No lock-in, no vendor dependency. I'll document how to maintain it yourself if you want.
