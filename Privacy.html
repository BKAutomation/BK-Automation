Privacy Policy

BK Automation  ·  Last updated: April 2026  ·  Complies with the NZ Privacy Act 2020

The short version: your data runs through your own accounts. Nothing is stored on BK Automation servers. I build the connections — you keep the data.

Who we are

BK Automation is a sole-trader business based in Hawke's Bay, New Zealand. We build workflow automations for NZ small businesses and tradies.

What information we collect

When you fill out the contact form on this website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your message

This information is delivered directly to us via EmailJS and used solely to respond to your enquiry. It is not stored in any database or third-party CRM.

How automations handle your customer data

When we build automations for your business, all data (customer enquiries, invoices, replies) flows exclusively through your own accounts — your Gmail, your Xero, your Google Sheets. BK Automation does not store, access, or process your customer data on our own servers at any point.

You retain full ownership and control of all data at all times.

Third-party tools

Automations may use the following third-party services, all of which have their own privacy policies:

  • Google (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Drive)
  • Xero (accounting)
  • Make / Integromat (automation platform)
  • Anthropic Claude API (AI replies)
  • Facebook / Meta (lead capture, where applicable)

Where data is processed overseas (e.g. through Google or Anthropic), we ensure this is handled in line with the NZ Privacy Act 2020's cross-border disclosure rules.

Your rights under the NZ Privacy Act 2020

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us directly.

Data breaches

In the event of a serious privacy breach, we will notify affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner within 72 hours, as required by the NZ Privacy Act 2020.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, contact Bhuwan Kshetri at BK Automation via the contact form on our website.
